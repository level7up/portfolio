# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: potential clients seeking senior backend/full-stack contract work delivered through Nuvexa Digital, plus recruiters and engineering managers in Egypt, the Gulf (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar) and Germany evaluating AbdulAlim for senior roles. They skim fast, on a laptop, between candidates or proposals, and hire on credibility, not polish.

## Product Purpose

A single-page portfolio that wins contract work via Nuvexa Digital and stays credible for senior employment. Success: a visitor understands the multi-tenant ERP depth within seconds, believes the cross-border delivery record, and contacts AbdulAlim (direct channels, no fake forms).

## Positioning

The engineer behind Gdawel — a multi-tenant ERP serving 150+ businesses in Saudi Arabia with tenant-isolated databases — who also publishes: seven open-source Laravel/Node packages (ZATCA e-invoicing, OpenRouter AI client, WhatsApp Business, MCP servers). A neighboring portfolio could not truthfully claim this production record; Nuvexa Digital turns it into a delivery vehicle for clients.

## Operating Context

Evaluation happens resume-first: claims must match `resume.md.txt` plus user-confirmed facts (Nuvexa Digital Co-founder & CEO, Present; start date unknown — do not invent). Visitors cross-check GitHub (github.com/level7up) and live systems (gdawel.app, claimizer.com, khurdahapp.com, darabny.com).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 15 + Tailwind CSS 3 + framer-motion; single page, no dark mode — the design is pinned to a light ground by user decision.
- English copy, professional and factual — no inflated marketing language.
- Contact is direct channels only (email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub); the simulated-submit contact form was removed as dishonest.
- Nuvexa Digital blurb (user-approved): delivering cloud and AI-enabled software solutions for businesses.

## Brand Commitments

- Light theme is binding: the site renders in white/light paper tones only; do not reintroduce a dark theme or a theme toggle.
- The passport visual world (burgundy identity, security-paper ground, ink stamps) is the committed identity; MRZ strips and "Endorsed/Endorsements" labels were explicitly removed by the user — do not reinstate them.

## Evidence on Hand

- `resume.md.txt` — the resume of record; all claims trace to it or to user-confirmed facts.
- Live systems: gdawel.app, claimizer.com, pasma.hashstudio.dev, khurdahapp.com, eyetracker.hashstudio.dev, darabny.com, ma3loma-front.vercel.app.
- Open source: 7 packages — openrouter-client (Packagist), trello-mcp (GitHub), zatca-laravel, whatsapp-bot, Dashboard Metronic, CMS, E-commerce.
- No personal photo asset exists; the data page uses typographic fields. Do not fabricate testimonials, client logos, or metrics beyond the resume.

## Product Principles

1. Claims match the resume — nothing invented, nothing inflated.
2. The work leads: systems shipped and packages published outrank adjectives.
3. Contact is honest and effortless: real channels, immediate.
4. One document: the site stays a single light-ground page in the passport world.
5. Contract-work first: Nuvexa Digital is the delivery vehicle the site sells through.
